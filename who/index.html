Who we are

Experience is important, but it’s only the beginning.

The Flattery Films team brings decades of experience across documentary filmmaking, television, and cinematic branded storytelling, collectively earning more than 20 Emmy nominations, 9 wins, and over a billion views worldwide.

Our filmmakers have contributed to some of television’s most recognizable productions, including The Big Bang Theory, Two and a Half Men, and The Ellen DeGeneres Show, while also creating original docuseries such as Wild Response and Thin Space.

From feature-length documentaries to short-form branded content, we bring the same creative vision and technical expertise to every project.

Our experience may span genres and formats, but what unites us is a shared belief that extraordinary filmmaking begins with a story worth telling.

Founding Partner · Writer/Director

Wil Beaucher is a passionate writer/director who has shaped narratives for over two decades. With a rich tapestry of experience, Wil is a seasoned architect of both rewriting existing projects and creating original film and television scripts. His storytelling prowess goes beyond fiction, extending into the dynamic realm of docu-style advertising where he crafts and directs compelling ad campaigns through personal connection. Founding Partner · Executive Producer

Dani Behr

Dani Behr, a seasoned entertainment veteran, boasts an illustrious career spanning over 35 years.

Her journey began with acting with acclaimed theatre show producer Cameron Mackintosh, signing a record deal with WEA shortly after with her All-Girl Pop Group and then becoming a household name as a live TV Host. She achieved cult status in her native UK for her late-night show and was recognized with the prestigious ‘Best TV Presenter’ award. Dani continued to thrive across various media platforms, serving as a TV Host, Radio Host, Voice Over Artist, and TV Producer ever since. She has left her mark across all TV genres, collaborating with renowned networks such as ABC, NBCU, FOX, BBC, C4, and ITV.

Dubbed the ‘Voice Over Queen’, Dani has lent her voice to numerous brands including the BBC, Virgin Atlantic, and the iconic video game Lara Croft.

The Drive-time Radio Host on KISS FM, shared the stage and screen with acclaimed actors like Kate Winslet, Joseph Fiennes, Cate Blanchett, and Oprah Winfrey. Performed at legendary venues such as Wembley Stadium, The Old Vic, and Madison Square Gardens. danibehr.com

Currently, Dani serves as the Founder & Executive Producer of DBI Entertainment, a dynamic TV & Film Production company dedicated to creating scripted and non-scripted projects for the global marketplace. dbi-ent.com

Additionally, she has recently co-founded Flattery Films, a content creation production company specializing in storytelling through branded documentaries with her award-winning team.
